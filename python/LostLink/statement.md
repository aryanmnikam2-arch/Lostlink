# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students can lose personal belongings around campus and may not have a central system for reporting, searching and tracking those items.

## Scope
LostLink provides a simple campus-focused platform for submitting and searching lost/found item reports. Future versions can add authentication, claims, matching and administration.

## Target Users
- Students
- Faculty/staff
- Campus administrators

## High-Level Features
- Lost/found reporting
- Search
- Item status tracking
- Future claim and matching functionality
